Fuchsia ‘Mrs Popple’

Fuchsia

BorderHardy shrub

A reliable hardy fuchsia with scarlet sepals and a violet-purple skirt. It flowers from summer into autumn and returns year after year in the border.

Brazilian Fuchsia

Jacobinia pauciflora (Justicia floribunda)

Fence potTender evergreen

A graceful shrub with tubular red-orange flowers tipped in yellow. It isn’t frost hardy, so keep it in a pot where it can be moved to shelter for the winter.

More details on RHS →

Rose ‘My Lovely Mum’

Rosa (Fryflorida)

LawnRose

A special rose with golden-yellow blooms, planted in the lawn. Roses like a sunny spot, a regular feed and deadheading through the season to keep the blooms coming.

Daffodil ‘Tête-à-Tête’

Narcissus

Pots & bordersSpring bulb

A compact, cheerful dwarf daffodil with golden yellow flowers, often more than one per stem. Flowers early in spring and is perfect for pots.

Crocus tommasinianus

Crocus

Pots & bordersSpring bulb

One of the earliest signs of spring, with slender lilac-purple flowers that open in sunshine. It naturalises well and spreads gradually over the years.
